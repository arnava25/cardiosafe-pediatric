# FAERS Secondary Analyses — Temporal Trend & Age Stratification
**CardioSafe Pediatric | June 2026**

---

## 1. Temporal Trend Analysis

Year-by-year ROR for key drug combinations in pediatric FAERS cases (2015–2024).
Tests whether pharmacovigilance signal strength has changed over time as
pediatric polypharmacy has grown.

### Methods
Per-year ROR computed for 8 key combinations using the same pediatric filter
(age < 18) and primary cardiac PT definition as the main FAERS analysis.
Minimum n=3 co-reported cases required for ROR computation.

### Key Findings

**MPH+SER**: Signal years: [2015, 2018, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024]. +52% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**MPH+ARI**: Signal years: [2023, 2024]. +102% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**MPH+QUE**: Signal years: [2015, 2018]. +20% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**ARI+GUA**: Signal years: [2021, 2022, 2023, 2024]. +20% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**QUE+GUA**: Signal years: [2015, 2018, 2019, 2021]. +71% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**QUE+FLU**: Signal years: [2015, 2016, 2019, 2020, 2024]. +247% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**MPH+RIS**: Signal years: [2024]. +28% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

**AMP+SER**: Signal years: [2017, 2021]. +196% change in co-reports 2015→2024.

## 2. Age Stratification Analysis

Comparison of ROR between children (6–12y) and adolescents (13–17y) for key
combinations to test whether cardiac AE signal is age-specific.

### Methods
Same pediatric cases split into two age bands. Cases with unknown age excluded
(as in main analysis). Same primary cardiac PT definition.

### Key Findings

**MPH+SER**: Children: ROR 43.71 [32.31–59.12] n=237. Adolescents: ROR 1.86 [0.85–4.05] n=325.

**MPH+ARI**: Children: ROR 23.71 [17.39–32.33] n=326. Adolescents: ROR 0.47 [0.10–2.35] n=291.

**MPH+QUE**: Children: ROR 16.45 [8.89–30.44] n=103. Adolescents: ROR 1.84 [0.53–6.42] n=126.

**ARI+GUA**: Children: ROR 21.72 [14.76–31.98] n=218. Adolescents: ROR 1.69 [0.48–5.90] n=137.

**QUE+GUA**: Children: ROR 32.15 [17.29–59.80] n=63. Adolescents: ROR 3.91 [1.10–13.87] n=60.

**QUE+FLU**: Children: ROR 3.75 [0.74–19.04] n=53. Adolescents: ROR 10.88 [7.92–14.93] n=414.

**MPH+RIS**: Children: ROR 0.86 [0.30–2.46] n=536. Adolescents: ROR 0.78 [0.22–2.70] n=295.

**AMP+SER**: Children: ROR 0.89 [0.06–14.41] n=73. Adolescents: ROR 3.12 [1.73–5.63] n=348.

---

## Limitations

- Age is unknown for ~43% of FAERS cases; unknown-age cases excluded
  conservatively and may be enriched for pediatric patients
- Small n per year-group for rare combinations limits temporal analysis
  statistical power
- Reporting patterns may have changed over time (FDA MedWatch campaigns,
  EHR integration) independent of true risk changes
- Age band definitions (6–12 vs 13–17) are arbitrary; puberty onset varies
