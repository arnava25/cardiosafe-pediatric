CYP2D6 genotype
CYP2D6 is a liver enzyme that clears several drugs in this panel (aripiprazole, risperidone, nortriptyline, imipramine). About 7–10% of people are poor metabolizers — their drug levels run 1.75–2× higher than expected. Only relevant if the patient has been genotyped.
